About Bentley

Warm, grounded therapy
with room for your full experience.

Hi, I’m Bentley. I’m someone who naturally looks beneath the surface of things. I’ve always been drawn to understanding people, why we feel the way we do, how our experiences shape us, and what it really means to live in a way that feels true to ourselves.

Outside of my work, I value quiet, reflective moments just as much as connection. I love conversations that feel real and meaningful, where you don’t have to filter yourself or pretend to be anything you’re not.

My path into this work hasn’t just been academic, it’s been personal. I understand what it’s like to question yourself, feel misunderstood, or try to make sense of experiences that don’t quite fit into neat boxes. Those experiences shape how I show up: with openness, curiosity, and deep respect for each person’s reality.

People often describe me as warm, grounded, and easy to talk to. I care deeply about helping others feel more like themselves again.

FAQ

Questions you may be wondering about.

What if I’ve had a bad experience with therapy before?

Many clients come in feeling cautious or unsure because therapy hasn’t felt safe or helpful in the past. We’ll move at a pace that feels respectful, with space to talk about what didn’t work before and what you need now.

Who do you work with?

I work with adults, adolescents, and couples using a collaborative, respectful approach.

What does an initial session look like?

The first session functions much like an ongoing session. We’ll spend time getting to know what brings you in, what you’re hoping for, and whether this approach feels like a good fit.

Do you accept insurance?

Insurance is not accepted at this time. Clients may choose to submit a superbill for possible out-of-network reimbursement, but reimbursement is not guaranteed.

Are services offered in person or online?

Sessions are currently offered via secure telehealth to clients located in Florida.

Do you provide crisis or emergency services?

No. Aligned & Empowered does not provide emergency services. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, call 911, go to the nearest emergency room, or call/text 988.

What is your licensure status?

I am a Registered Mental Health Counselor Intern in the state of Florida and practice under qualified supervision.
